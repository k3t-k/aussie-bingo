🦘 Aussie Bingo: Spicy Version

18+ spicy version with the crude slang mixed in. Tap a word when you hear it. Get 5 in a row (or a full house) and yell “Ripper!”

🍻 Drinking games

1. Bingo Sips

  • Sip every time you mark a square.
  • Complete a line: everyone else drinks 3 sips.
  • Full house: the winner nominates someone to skull.

2. Dinkum or Furphy?

  • Take turns picking a word from your card and giving a definition: either the real one or a made-up one.
  • Everyone votes “Fair dinkum!” or “Furphy!” at the same time.
  • Wrong voters drink; if everyone is fooled, the bluffer gives out 3 sips.

3. Shorten It, Mate

  • Go round the circle shortening a word the Aussie way (brekky, servo, arvo) or saying a slang word. No repeats.
  • Hesitate, repeat or blank: drink.
  • Anyone who says “mate” during the game drinks too.

Please drink responsibly, and water counts as a “cold one” too.
